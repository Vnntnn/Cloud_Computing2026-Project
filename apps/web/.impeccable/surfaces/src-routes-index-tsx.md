# Surface brief: home (`/`)

Mode: Persuade (the visitor decides to look at events, search, or sign up to host). Brief pinned by the user: "Cinematic Editorial Event Discovery".

Audience: Thai-speaking public on phones first; English equal. Job: see what is on, search, open an event. Proof: only real data from the events query; photographs are atmosphere, never claimed as Eventide events.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The date is the poster. A night-market photograph, ink-dark ground and giant date numerals make a cultural-magazine "what's on" cover; every event is a numeral before it is a card. Refuses the split hero of copy beside collage, the ticket ornament, and the grid of identical rounded cards.

OWN-WORLD: Night ink (dusk teal-black) with marigold; six market-tent poster colourways (vermilion, magenta, indigo, marigold, green, ink) that colour-code categories across posters, index rows and chips. Google Sans bold, display up to 6rem, tight tracking, giant tabular numerals. Wave-line motif from the logo as the only texture. Sharp 0 to 4px edges on editorial blocks, no stacked rounded cards. Photograph graded into the ink, never a framed picture.

STORY: Visitor sees a lit night market, reads "find the event, hold the ticket", types in the search field, and glimpses the next three real events as a programme. Below: the coming events as a billboard plus a ledger, a typographic index of kinds, then an invitation to host.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed ink hero. Headline left (2 lines, ~6rem on desktop) overlapping a tall photo plate that bleeds off the right edge and fades into the ink; search field directly under the headline (max 36rem, white, amber submit); a programme strip pinned to the hero's base: three next events as numeral, title, venue. Mobile: photo band on top, headline over its fade, search by 560px, programme as three rows below.

FORM: Editorial cover plus listings ledger (candidate 1 of 1, user-pinned). seed: none (direction pinned by the brief, no roll run)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
